How I work

Diagnosis before doctrine.

Sometimes the answer is a new system. Sometimes it is a targeted change to one that already works. The job is to find the real constraint and tie the fix to a commercial result.

The operating rule underneath it, since Amazon: acquire the right customers, help them win with your product, and make getting value easier. Done well, the cost of serving a customer stops tracking the revenue they produce.

  1. 01

    See where value is moving

    Read the technology shift, the buyer behavior behind it, and the commercial consequence before choosing a playbook.

  2. 02

    Make the bet

    Decide what must change, what should stay intact, and where the company can build an advantage early.

  3. 03

    Build the operating system

    Form follows function. Positioning, people, process, product surfaces, pricing, channels, and measurement all take their shape from the commercial job, not from an org chart template.

Current point of view

AI is the newest inflection point, and it arrives market by market.

The Serviceable Line is how far your customers, and increasingly their AI, get with your offering on their own. Past it, you either pay for a person to step in or lose revenue, and most often these are quiet losses, the ones your funnel never records.
