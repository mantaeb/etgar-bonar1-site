Current point of view

The part of the customer journey your team never sees is getting larger.

Before purchase, buyers use AI to research, compare, and plan an integration. After it, that same AI increasingly connects the product, operates it, and troubleshoots it.

The cost-to-serve line is the boundary between what your product, content, and interfaces can carry on their own, and what still costs you a person. There are five of these lines, and each moves at a different speed.
